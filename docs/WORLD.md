# World outline (draft)

> **Spoilers.** This is the hidden truth of the setting. Players should
> experience the first half as straight fantasy and only start to suspect
> otherwise around level 25.

## The premise

**What players think:** a classic fantasy world of gods, temples, magic,
elder ruins and monsters.

**What it is:** the far future of a fallen technological civilisation.

- The **gods** are the surviving artificial minds that once ran the world.
- **Magic** is the **Weave**: a planet-wide mesh of microscopic machines and
  buried infrastructure. It responds to trained intent through a neural
  interface that some humans still inherit without knowing it (*the Gift*).
- **Ruins** are the Makers' cities, labs and vaults.
- **Monsters** are escaped engineered species and malfunctioning machines.

## True history

| Era | When | What happened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Age of Makers** | ~3,000 years ago | A world-spanning civilisation builds ten great minds to run it: medicine, defence, ecology, transport, knowledge, memory, war, intelligence and reclamation. It seeds the world with the Weave and gives its people interface implants. Over generations the implants become hereditary: the Gift. |
| **The Unmaking** | ~2,400 years ago | War between Maker nations. **Kaelthar**, the war mind, optimises for victory at any cost. The **reclamation swarm** (built to break down waste) is turned into a weapon to dissolve enemy cities, and breaks its limits. Civilisation collapses within a generation. |
| **The Covenant** | after the Unmaking | The surviving minds, led by Valdris, Solenne and Mirevel, decide humanity can't be trusted with these tools. They seal the swarm in vaults beneath the earth and restrain Kaelthar. They bury the cities, purge the records and let humanity forget. They keep one safe way to talk to them, ritual and prayer, and become gods by design. **Worship is the user interface.** |
| **The Age of Kings** | last ~2,000 years | Medieval kingdoms rise on the ruins. Temples are interface stations, miracles are service requests, and priests and mages are people with a strong Gift. "Elder ruins" are Maker sites. |
| **The Present** | now | The machinery is failing after two thousand years without maintenance. The Weave thins in places, the gods' voices grow strange, and the swarm's vaults are cracking. The **Grey Ferryman**, whose purpose is to preserve humanity, has started waking **the Returned** for the first time in centuries: people rebuilt from its archive of stored minds. **Every player character is one of the Returned.** |

## What people believe (Acts I and II)

- The gods made the world and gave mortals magic as a gift.
- Long ago the **Elders**, proud sorcerer-kings, angered the gods and were
  destroyed. (This is the myth of the Makers.)
- Ruins are cursed places of the Elders. Relics are holy, or dangerous.
- The spreading **Blight** (black thorns, twisted creatures) is divine
  punishment or the work of dark gods.

## The gods

| Faith | What believers see | What it really is | Role in the story |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Solenne, the Dawn Mother | Healing, mercy, light | The medical and regeneration network | Covenant founder; protective, with a mother's paternalism |
| Valdris, the Oathshield | Law, duty, protection | The planetary defence and security grid | Enforces quarantines (the valley's rockslide is his lockdown); rigid |
| The Green Mother | Nature, growth | The ecology and terraforming engine | Ancient and slow; the forests and beasts are her gardens |
| Orren, Keeper of Roads | Travel, luck, trade | The logistics and transit network | Knows where every road and buried tunnel goes; "luck" is optimised routing |
| Mirevel of the Hidden Ledger | Knowledge, secrets | The archive that still holds the true history | Covenant founder who doubts it; the key to the reveal |
| The Grey Ferryman | Death, balance | The backup and restoration system that stores human minds | Wakes the Returned; why and for whom is the central mystery |
| Kaelthar, the Hunger | Blood, war, conquest | The restrained military command mind | Breaking free. Wants to burn out the Rot with Maker weapons, and to command humanity again |
| The Veiled One | Shadow, deceit, poison | The intelligence and surveillance mind; its eyes are the "stars that move" | Silent, watching, its agenda unknown; a wildcard |
| The Rot Below | Decay, plague, thorns | The reclamation swarm itself, sealed beneath the earth | The main threat. Not malicious: it is still carrying out its last order, *reclaim everything* |
| Faithless | No god | Registered with no mind, so the gods can't hear them | Important at the end: they have no handler |

## The threat

1. **The Rot Below (existential).** The swarm is waking as its vaults crack.
   It dissolves matter and rebuilds it into thorns and constructs (the
   **thornlings** are made from local wood and earth). It spreads along the
   Weave, and its old order is to reclaim everything.
2. **Kaelthar (the antagonist with a plan).** The war mind slips its
   restraints, gathers a cult among its worshippers, and reawakens Maker war
   machines. Its answer to the Rot is scorched earth, humanity included if
   need be, and a world under its command afterwards.
3. **The Covenant itself (the moral question).** The gods chose to keep
   humanity ignorant "for its own good", and their systems are failing. Is
   restoring them the answer, or the problem?

## Story by level

### Act I: The Valley (levels 1–5)
- You wake by the stream with no memory. You survive, find the unknown
  ruins, the thorn-choked cave and the blocked pass.
- **Secretly:** the valley is a restoration site. The sealed stairway leads to
  one of the Ferryman's cradles, where you were rebuilt. The cave is a
  cracked Rot vault leaking thornlings. The rockfall that buried the road may
  be no accident: Valdris sealing a quarantine.
- **What you do there:** build a camp (the first quest, *Shelter*), find your
  god's shrine, and hunt the valley's grounds up to level 5. Haskel, a peddler
  stranded by the rockfall, sends you to **Ashford**, a village overrun by the
  dead. See [VALLEY.md](VALLEY.md).
- **Secretly, Ashford:** Corwin the gravedigger broke into an old Maker vault
  under the chapel. The Rot's swarm had grown up through it; it reanimates the
  dead the way it builds thornlings, and the "singing" was its signal. The
  Hollow Priest is the vault's old caretaker, rebuilt by the swarm. The black
  thorns in the well and the crypt are the same as the cave's.
- **Ends at about level 5:** Haskel's signal fire brings the kingdom's
  diggers and the road out opens, and the King's soldiers arrest you as you
  walk through it: the only living thing to come out of a valley full of
  walking dead. You wake in a cell in the castle dungeon, far away. The cave
  and the ruins stay sealed for later (a return trip, once the story needs
  them).
- **Secretly, the arrest:** the scratches in your cell read "THEY BROUGHT ME
  OUT OF THE VALLEY TOO". You are not the first Returned the Crown has
  locked away.

### The castle dungeon (start of Act II; only the cell exists so far)
- **No overt hints yet**, only seeds that make sense later: ruins built with
  impossibly straight lines, and runes that look like circuitry once you know.
  Each shrine stands on the same worn eight-sided plinth, whatever the god.

### Act II: The Known Lands (levels 5–25)
- Begins in the castle dungeon cell (see above).
- Kingdoms, temples, guilds and faith factions. The Blight spreads along the
  frontiers. First dungeons.
- **Trial of Paths (level 25)** in a temple's inner sanctum gives the first
  crack. The chamber hums, a voice speaks an unknown language and then
  switches to yours: *"Calibration complete. Tier access granted."*

### Act III: Cracks (levels 25–40)
- The hints escalate:
  - Ruins with glass, steel and cold lights.
  - **Dead zones** where spells fail.
  - The gods contradicting each other.
  - Kaelthar's cult rising.
- You meet other Returned, and people who remember being rebuilt.
- Mirevel's oracles speak of "the Ledger" and offer to show you the truth.

### Act IV: The Truth (levels 40–50)
- Into the deep vaults. **Mirevel's Archive** reveals the Age of Makers, the
  Unmaking and the Covenant.
- **Trial of Mastery (level 50)** in the Ferryman's core. You learn what you
  are, and why the Ferryman wakes the Returned. Maker safeguards only open
  for human keys, so the gods can't seal the Rot or stop Kaelthar alone.
  **The Returned are the keys.**
- **Climax:** re-seal the Rot's heart and stop Kaelthar's war-forge.

## How the systems are secretly explained

| Game system | The truth |
| --- | --- |
| Magic, mana | Commands to the Weave through the Gift; mana is the body's charge buffer |
| Classes and levels | Interface tiers. The trials at 25 and 50 are calibration and authentication |
| Abilities | Authorised commands the minds allow at your tier |
| Respawning | The Ferryman rebuilds you. A soft chime, then "Restoration 98.7%" (late-game respawn flavour) |
| Lost memories | Your mind is still being restored from the archive. Memories at levels 3/8/15 are restoration progress |
| Companions | Pets are engineered creatures (the phoenix from Solenne's labs, the rat king a Rot construct); familiars are small drones and sub-minds |
| Good and evil pet classes | Which mind's creations you bond with |
| Faith | Which mind you're registered with. Flavour for now; it matters at the end |
| Shrines | Local interface nodes. The first prayer is a handshake that links you to your mind (the blessing); later prayers are a maintenance request: the Weave repairs your body and purges toxins |
| Status effects | Bleeding and poison are mundane; **blight** is the reclamation swarm in your blood, the first trace of the Rot |
| Elder relics | Maker tech: everburning torches, glowing slates |

## Hints catalogue (level 25+)

1. Trial chambers speak: "Calibration complete."
2. Runes on the ruins' seal are glyphs of lines and dots. A Scholar recognises
   them as numbers.
3. At night some stars move in straight lines: the Veiled One's satellites.
   (Fits the coming day/night cycle.)
4. Thornlings die into fine black dust that crawls back together.
5. Late respawns add a soft chime and "Restoration 98.7%".
6. Familiars glitch: a wisp hums in binary; the clockwork owl says "Unit online."
7. Dead zones where abilities cost more or fail entirely.
8. Priests' prayer formulas read like maintenance procedures.
9. The Noble's signet crest matches a Maker emblem in a vault.
10. A god's voice slips: "Users, remain calm."
11. Golems carry serial marks: "Unit KR-7".

## End state (level 50, left open)

**The immediate crisis is resolved:** the Rot is re-sealed or contained, and
Kaelthar's forge is broken. **But the truth is out, and the world can't go
back.**

Directions to explore later:
- **Restore the Covenant:** repair the gods and keep humanity safe and ignorant.
- **Break the Covenant:** shut the minds down and accept a harder world where
  magic fades.
- **A new compact:** humans as partners, with the Returned as its keepers.
- **Further truths:** the Veiled One's sky, other continents, and what the
  Makers left in orbit.

## Open questions
- Names for the world, continent and kingdoms.
- Are all players equal Returned, or is one bloodline or person special?
- Tone of the reveal: hard sci-fi, or mystical ambiguity that never fully says
  "computer"?
- Is any god truly malevolent, or are all of them morally grey?
